My Shop

A starter kit for changing the flower shop.

1. Open mod.json in any text editor. Change "yourname" in "id" and "authors"
   to your own name (lower case letters, digits and dashes), and give the mod
   a "name" and "description" of your own.
2. Open shops.json. Change the shop's name, hours, greeting, stock line and
   stock. Each good is an item number and a price in Bells. The item number is
   a string like "0x2900". Leave "price" out for the usual price. A shop has
   1 to 6 goods.
3. ZIP the folder with Finder Compress or Windows Send to. Zipping the files
   inside it works too.
   In the game, open Settings, Mods. Under Install from a file, choose
   Choose a file. Then choose Restart now.
4. Visit the flower shop during your opening hours.

Guide and item numbers: https://playaconline.com/modding/guides/flower-shop/
