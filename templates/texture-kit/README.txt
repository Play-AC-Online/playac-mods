My Textures

A starter kit for a texture mod.

Installed as it is, this kit turns the town grass into a bold two-green
check, from spring to autumn. You can see a texture mod work before you paint
anything. The pictures are in textures/. They are three copies of one 64x64
picture, one for each name the game draws the grass with. Each name has $ in
place of a palette, so the picture is drawn by day and by night.

Start your own mod from this kit. Find the names of the textures you want
in the model viewer: https://playaconline.com/viewer/ (pick a model, press
Textures, then Copy name). It shows the textures of models, such as
furniture, trees, buildings and villagers.

1. Open mod.json in any text editor. Change "yourname" in "id" and "authors"
   to your own name (lower case letters, digits and dashes), and give the mod
   a "name" and "description" of your own.
2. Paint over the pictures in textures/, or delete them and put your own
   .dds or .png files there, each under the exact name you copied.
3. ZIP the folder with Finder Compress or Windows Send to. Zipping the files
   inside it works too.
   In the game, open Settings, Mods. Under Install from a file, choose
   Choose a file. Then choose Restart now.

Guide: https://playaconline.com/modding/guides/textures/
